Help and questions

Most problems have a quick fix. Find what you see below.

Something isn’t right

What you seeWhat to do
No sound at the adhanAlerts start as Message only; choose a sound (chapter 5). Tap the screen once after opening the app. Check the volume and that the tablet is not muted.
Tap to enable sound keeps appearingThe browser needs one tap after the app opens. Tap the button and the alert plays.
The screen goes darkTap Keep screen on, and set a long screen timeout or Auto-Lock to Never (chapter 1).
The page turns amber or dark at nightThat is Dim automatically. Change or turn it off in chapter 6, or tap Bright.
Adhan times differ from my masjidCheck the method and Asr setting, then adjust single prayers by a few minutes (chapter 2).
Iqama times look oldTap Sync now on the Today screen. Away from ISCJ, set your own (chapter 4).
The Hijri date is a day outMove it a day in Settings › Location (chapter 2).
Quran or adhkar won’t playThey stream from the internet. Check the Wi-Fi.
My settings have goneSettings live in the browser on this tablet. Clearing the browser’s data, or opening the app in a different browser, starts afresh.

Questions

Does it work without the internet?

The clock keeps running with the last times it read, and the alerts still go off, though the adhan recording may not play. Weather, Quran and adhkar recordings need the internet.

Do I need an account? Does it cost anything?

No account, no cost, no ads. It runs in the web browser.

What does it keep about me?

Your settings, favourites and playlists stay on this tablet. Your location is used only to work out prayer times and fetch the weather.

Can I use it on my phone too?

Yes. It works the same on a phone. Settings on the phone and the tablet are kept separately.

Where do the times, recitations and duas come from?

Every source is listed under Settings › About, under Sources & credits.

Still stuck?

Use the contact form on the portfolio site, and say what you see and which tablet you use.
