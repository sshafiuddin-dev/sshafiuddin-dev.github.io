      Sources & credits

      Quran

      Recitations, the list of reciters and riwayat, and surah names come from mp3quran.net. Each recitation is by the reciter named in the player and streams from mp3quran.net’s servers.

      Adhkar and duas

      From Hisn al-Muslim (Fortress of the Muslim) by Saʿid ibn ʿAli ibn Wahf al-Qahtani. The Arabic text, transliteration, English translation and Arabic recordings are from hisnmuslim.com. Each dua on the Today tab names its chapter, and the Quran verse or hadith where known.

      Adhan and alert sounds

      Adhan recordings, by the muezzins named under Adhan and alerts, come with the open-source Adhan Clock. The spoken Bismillah is hisnmuslim.com’s recording from Hisn al-Muslim. The soft beep is made in the app.

      Prayer and iqama times

      Prayer times are calculated with PrayTimes.org by Hamid Zarrabi-Zadeh (LGPL 3.0), as adapted with its calculation methods in Adhan Clock (MIT). Iqama and Jumuʿah times near the masjid come from the Islamic Society of Central Jersey. Check with your masjid before relying on them.

      Weather and places

      Weather by Open-Meteo.com (CC BY 4.0). Air quality from Open-Meteo, using Copernicus Atmosphere Monitoring Service information. Place search by Open-Meteo, using GeoNames (CC BY 4.0).

      daisyUI and Tailwind CSS (MIT), Motion (MIT). Fonts: Amiri, Bricolage Grotesque and IBM Plex Sans, from Google Fonts (SIL Open Font License 1.1).
