Iqama Times guide

Give an old tablet or iPad a new job.

Iqama Times turns a spare tablet into a prayer hub for your home: a large clock with today’s adhan and iqama times, alerts, the weather, a daily dua, adhkar and Quran recitation. This guide shows you how to set it up and how to use every screen.

Good to know

  • It runs in the web browser. There is no account to make and nothing to buy.
  • Your settings stay on the tablet you set them on.
  • Near ISCJ in Monmouth Junction, NJ, iqama times come from the masjid’s own timetable. Anywhere else, you set your own.
