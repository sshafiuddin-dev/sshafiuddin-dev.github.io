All 132 chapters of Hisn al-Muslim to read and listen to, and the whole month’s adhan times on one page.

Adhkar

The Adhkar tab.
  1. Search by a word in the chapter’s title, such as sleep or travel.
  2. Tap a chapter to open it and read its duas.
  3. Chapter plays the whole chapter, recited in Arabic.
A chapter, opened.
  1. Which dua of the chapter this is.
  2. How many times to say it.
  3. Play recites just this dua.

Month

The Month tab.
  1. Share this month sends the whole timetable by message or any app you choose.
  2. The Hijri date for each day. The month’s name shows where a new Hijri month starts.
  3. Fridays are in red. Today’s row is marked too.

Good to know

  • The Month tab shows adhan times only. Iqama times are on the Today screen.
  • Adhkar recordings come from hisnmuslim.com and need the internet. The text is kept on the tablet.
