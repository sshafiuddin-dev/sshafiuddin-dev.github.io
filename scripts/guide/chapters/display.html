Choose how the clock looks, and how bright the screen is by day and at night. All of it is under Settings › Display.

Clock styles

Six ways to show the time and today’s prayers. Pick one under Clock style.

Masjid board: the time in large print, the prayers in columns like the board at the masjid. 24-hour dial: the whole day as a ring, with a countdown in the middle. Analog clock face: hands, with the coming prayers marked round the edge. Sun path: where the sun is between sunrise and Maghrib. Day timeline: the day as a list, with iqama times on the right. Bedside, low glare: amber on black, for a bedroom.

Look and brightness

The top of the Display page.
  1. Clock style: the six styles above.
  2. Look: Printed timetable (paper and ink), Handwritten (blue pen on a ruled sheet), or High contrast (black and white, easiest to read from afar).
  3. Brightness now: Bright, Soft, Dim or Darkest. The Bright button at the top of every screen does the same.

Night, duas and full screen

Further down the Display page.
  1. Dim automatically: After Isha, After Maghrib, or Off. The screen goes bright again at sunrise.
  2. At night: how dim it goes: Soft, Dim or Darkest.
  3. Show duas and adhkar and Show transliteration turn the dua on the Today screen, and its English letters, on or off.
  4. Full screen layout: Times only, Clock, prayers and dua, or Automatic, which shows times only on small screens.

Good to know

  • Arabic text size (Small, Medium, Large) is at the bottom of the page, with Keep screen on.
  • A web page cannot turn down the tablet’s backlight. Dim and Darkest darken the page itself. For a darker room, lower the tablet’s own brightness too.
