Questions

Does it work without the internet?

The clock keeps running with the last times it read, and the alerts still go off, though the adhan recording may not play. Weather, Quran and adhkar recordings need the internet.

Do I need an account? Does it cost anything?

No account, no cost, no ads. It runs in the web browser.

What does it keep about me, and what does it send?

Your settings, favourites and playlists are saved only on this tablet, in its browser. There is no account. Prayer times are worked out on the tablet itself. The app sends out only:

  • your place’s latitude and longitude to Open-Meteo, for the weather (rounded to about 100 m if you use your location), and any city name you search for;
  • a request to this site’s own server for the ISCJ iqama times, with nothing about you in it;
  • requests to mp3quran.net and hisnmuslim.com when you play a recitation or a dua, and to Google Fonts for the lettering.

The full list is under Settings › About › What leaves this device.

Can I use it on my phone too?

Yes. It works the same on a phone. Settings on the phone and the tablet are kept separately.

Where do the times, recitations and duas come from?

Every source is listed under Settings › About, under Sources & credits.
